## Batches

1. H22/N_1234567: 200 videos from Channel M; Column: C/accession number
2. NFWA 12 : 200 videos from rest of collection; Column: BX/File Name

CM: This collection consists of the entire news and music output from one of the early examples of a local TV station, Channel M for Manchester, which closed down in 2012. Alongside the extremely rich resource of the news coverage, the collection also features early TV performances from a number of bands including Dutch Uncles, Everything Everything and Friendly Fires. There are some complete news programmes, but the majority of the news coverage is rushes tapes and raw footage.  The channel also had a lot of music content, frequently promoting new and unsigned bands alongside more established acts.  1,689 tapes were digitised as part of H22, around 35% of the collection.  One of the issues with this collection is that due to a printing error on the barcodes, **label information in the metadata does not match up with the file content**

The other content is a mixture of catalogued items from our main film and video collection – wanted to supply a broad range of material – professional and amateur, b/w and colour, sound and silent, and from film, video and born-digital originals.  Also some content where NWFA knows a lot about the background and the film-maker, alongside content where our knowledge is less complete.  Full catalogue metadata from our FileMaker Pro database is supplied in Excel format.
